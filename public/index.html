How It Works

Simple steps to start ordering premium agricultural products as a dealer

1

Register as Dealer

Create your dealer account in minutes. Provide your business details and get instant access to our wholesale catalogue.

2

Browse & Add to Cart

Explore our full range of products. Select your desired quantities (sold in bundles) and add them to your cart.

3

Checkout & Pay

Enter your delivery details, then pay via our secure payment gateway or direct bank transfer to our account.

Order Confirmed

Once payment is verified, your order is confirmed and dispatched. Track delivery status in your dashboard.
